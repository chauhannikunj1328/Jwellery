# Jwellery Research

Market and business research for the jewellery space.

## Start here
- `notes/launch-plan.md` – phased e-commerce launch plan and decisions log

## Structure
- `notes/market/` – market size, trends, pricing, channels
- `notes/competitors/` – one file per competitor (use `notes/competitors/_template.md`)
- `notes/customers/` – segments, interviews, survey findings
- `data/` – raw and cleaned datasets (CSV)
- `sources/` – `sources.md` log of every reference (URL, date accessed, summary)

## Conventions
- Every claim in notes cites an entry in `sources/sources.md`.
- Date-stamp notes (`YYYY-MM-DD`) since market data goes stale.
- Keep raw data untouched; put cleaned versions in a separate file.
