# Research questions

1. How large is the target jewellery market, and how fast is it growing?
2. Who are the main competitors and how are they priced?
3. Which customer segments buy, and through which channels?
4. What trends (lab-grown, personalisation, sustainability) matter?
5. Which product category (gold, silver, fashion, lab-grown) best fits our capital, sourcing and target customer?
6. Which 1–2 countries should we test first, given demand, duties and shipping?
7. What niche can we own online, and at what price range and margin?

See `notes/launch-plan.md` for how these feed the launch.
